1. Overview

Photoest lets you take photos, import photos, create selective-colour edits, add stickers or shapes, and save final images. The current app does not require an account and does not operate a self-owned server that uploads or stores your photos.

2. Information We Access or Process

  • Camera: used only to capture photos inside the app.
  • Photo Library: used to import photos you choose and save edited results.
  • Photos and edits: processed locally on your device for filters, selective colour regions, stickers, shapes, and saved output.
  • Feedback email: if you contact us, your email address and message are sent through your mail app and received by us.

Photoest does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, precise location, contacts, payment information, or account credentials.

3. Third Parties

Photoest uses Apple platform frameworks for camera, photos, haptics, and system features. If you download Photoest from the App Store, Apple may process download, crash, diagnostic, and analytics data according to Apple's policies and your device settings. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process standard web request logs when you visit it.

4. Children

Photoest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through feedback email, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

5. Your Choices

  • You can deny camera or photo library permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete photos saved by Photoest from the Photos app.
  • You can choose not to send feedback email.

6. Service Agreement

By using Photoest, you agree to use it only for lawful purposes and in a way that does not infringe the rights of others. You are responsible for the photos, edits, stickers, shapes, and other content you create, save, or share.

Photoest is provided "as is" and "as available." We do not guarantee that the app will be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for every purpose. To the maximum extent permitted by law, we are not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising from your use of the app.

We may update, suspend, or discontinue features as the product evolves. We may revise this policy and agreement from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
