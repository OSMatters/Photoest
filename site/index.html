1. Overview

Photoest is a camera and photo editing app that helps users create selective-colour photos, add shapes and stickers, and save finished images. The Photoest app is designed to process photos locally on your device. The current app does not require an account and does not operate a server that uploads or stores your photos.

Photoest does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, precise location, contacts, payment data, or account credentials.

3. Local Processing

Photo effects, subject selection, colour range editing, stickers, and saving are handled on your device. Imported or captured photos are not uploaded by Photoest to our servers.

4. Third Parties

Photoest uses Apple platform frameworks for camera, photos, haptics, and system features. If you download Photoest from the App Store, Apple may process download, crash, diagnostics, and analytics data according to Apple’s policies and your device settings. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process standard web request logs when you visit it.

5. Children

Photoest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through feedback email, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

6. Your Choices

  • You can deny camera or photo library permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete photos saved by Photoest from your Photos app.
  • You can choose not to send feedback email.

7. Data Security

Because Photoest currently processes photos locally, the main security controls are your device security, iOS permissions, and your own backup settings. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfect, but we keep the app’s data flow intentionally limited.

8. Service Agreement

By using Photoest, you agree to use it only for lawful purposes and in a way that does not infringe the rights of others. You are responsible for the photos, edits, stickers, and other content you create or share.

Photoest is provided “as is” and “as available.” We do not guarantee that the app will be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for every purpose. To the maximum extent permitted by law, we are not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising from your use of the app.

We may update, suspend, or discontinue features as the product evolves. We may revise this policy and agreement from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
