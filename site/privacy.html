Privacy Policy

Photoest is an iPhone camera and selective-colour photo editing app. It lets you take photos, import photos, edit colour regions, add stickers or shapes, and save final images. The current app does not require an account and does not operate a self-owned server that uploads or stores your photos.

Photoest accesses the camera to capture photos, the photo library to import photos you choose and save edited results, and local image data to render edits on your device. If you contact us by email, your email address and message are sent through your mail app and received by us.

Photoest does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, precise location, contacts, payment information, or account credentials. Apple may process App Store, crash, diagnostic, or analytics data according to Apple's policies and your device settings. GitHub may process standard web request logs for this hosted page.
